---
name: management-briefing
description: Prepare morning briefs, management decisions, shift handover and production comparisons.
---
Present changes since the prior period, unresolved high-severity issues, decisions needed, accountable owners and due dates where evidenced. State exact reporting windows and units. Normalize production rates when window lengths differ; never compare a seven-day total with a monthly total as equivalent. Surface summaries that the audience can access; hierarchy alone does not grant raw workspace access. Separate validated results from incomplete data. Keep private personal material out of shared reports.
